GAMING KNOWLEDGE

Helpful information for improving your game.

🎯 Aim & Accuracy

Keep your controls comfortable. Practise in the training area, pay attention to how your aim responds, and make small adjustments instead of changing every setting at once.

🛡️ Survival & Awareness

Watch the safe zone, use cover, pay attention to surroundings, and avoid rushing into fights without understanding your position.

🤝 Teamwork

Communicate with teammates, share information, stay close enough to support each other and plan rotations together.

⚙️ Settings

Comfortable sensitivity and button placement depend on your device, screen condition and play style. Use settings that feel controllable.
